1908, an Estonian fishing village. Taniel, a landless boy of fourteen, is sent for four years as apprentice to the shoemaker the village calls Kriuks. The fee is four years of waxed thread. He learns to twist thread, to last uppers, to boil pitch and to tan leather; to count with his eyes open under the ice; and, little by little, that the master swinging his arms in the yard each dawn is not shooing flies. 一九〇八年，一个爱沙尼亚渔村。十四岁没有土地的少年塔尼耶尔被送到人们叫作“咯吱”的鞋匠家当四年学徒，拜师钱只要四年的蜡线。他学会搓蜡线、绷帮、熬沥青、硝皮；学会在冰底下睁着眼睛数数；也一点一点明白，师傅每天清早在院子里挥动胳膊，不是在赶苍蝇。至于靴子为什么有时候唱、有时候一声不出，那是这所房子最后才肯交出来的东西。

RaamatustAbout the book关于这本书

„Sinu omad kriuksuvad. Tema omad laulavad.“

"Yours creak. His sing."

“你的是吱吱嘎嘎响。他的是唱。”

1908, Läänemere idakallas, üks Eesti kaluriküla. Neljateistkümneaastase Tanieli perel ei ole maad; nad elavad jõe ääres saunas. Jaaniöö kiigeplatsil kuuleb ta esimest korda seda häält: kõrge ja kuiv, iga sammuga kaks korda, täpselt lõõtspilli taktis. Kogu küla teab, kelle käe alt niisugused saapad tulevad. Kingseppa kutsuvad inimesed Kriuksuks.

Tema kohta räägitakse viit juttu: parun, sakslane, hull, joodik, hiinlane. Mida inimesed ei tea, seda arvavad nad seda kindlamalt.

Mihklipäeval saadetakse Taniel neljaks aastaks õpipoisiks. Õpiraha on ainult neli aastat pigitraati, muud midagi. Selles hallis majas õpib ta pigitraati keerutama, pealseid liistule tõmbama, puunaelu lööma, pigi keetma ja veisenahka parkima; õpib jää all lahtiste silmadega lugema; ja saab tasapisi aru, et meister, kes igal varahommikul õues käsi vehib, ei aja kärbseid. Miks saapad vahel laulavad ja vahel vaikivad — selle annab see maja välja kõige viimasena.

Aasta kestab mihklipäevast mihklipäevani. Selle lõpus on augustikuu mereudu, laev, mida siia lahte ei tohiks tulla, ja jälg, mille üks inimene jätab teise sisse.

1908, the eastern shore of the Baltic, an Estonian fishing village. Fourteen-year-old Taniel's family owns no land; they live in a sauna by the river. On the Midsummer Night swing ground he hears that sound for the first time: high and dry, twice with every step, right on the accordion's beat. The whole village knows whose hands make boots like these. People call the shoemaker Kriuks, "Creak".

There are five stories about him: a baron, a German, a madman, a drunk, a Chinese. What people do not know, they believe all the more firmly.

On Michaelmas, Taniel is sent away as an apprentice for four years. The fee is four years of waxed thread, nothing else. In that grey house he learns to twist waxed thread, to last uppers, to drive wooden pegs, to boil pitch and to tan a cowhide; to count with his eyes open under the ice; and, little by little, to understand that the master swinging his arms in the yard each dawn is not shooing flies. Why the boots sometimes sing and sometimes stay silent is the last thing the house is willing to give up.

The year runs from Michaelmas to Michaelmas. At its end are the August sea fog, a ship that should not be in this bay, and the mark one person leaves on another.

一九〇八年，波罗的海东岸，一个爱沙尼亚渔村。十四岁的塔尼耶尔一家没有土地，住在河边一间浴房里。仲夏夜的秋千场上，他第一次听见那个声音：又高又干，一响两下，正踩在手风琴的拍子上。全村都知道，这样的靴子出自谁的手。那个鞋匠，人们叫他“咯吱”。

关于他的说法有五种：男爵，德国人，疯子，酒鬼，中国人。人们不知道的，想得就更有把握。

米迦勒节那天，塔尼耶尔被送去当四年学徒。拜师钱只要四年的蜡线，别的什么都不要。在那所灰房子里，他学会搓蜡线、绷帮、钉木钉、熬沥青、硝一张牛皮；学会在冰底下睁着眼睛数数；也一点一点看明白，师傅每天清早在院子里挥动胳膊，不是在赶苍蝇。至于靴子为什么有时候唱、有时候一声不出，那是这所房子最后才肯交出来的东西。

这一年从米迦勒节走到米迦勒节。它的尽头是八月的海雾，一艘不该出现在这片海湾里的船，和一个人留在另一个人身上的印记。

AutoristAbout the author关于作者

Taniel Suvi on kingsepp (Kriuks), Shandongi Weifangi meistri Wang Xiuyuani õpilane alates 1991. aastast ja Taiji Tanglangquani 9. põlvkonna siseringi õpilane, vabasukeldumise instruktor ja allveepääste tegija. Raamatu käsitöö on tal kodust kaasas: vanavanaisa Artur oli Peipsi ääres Mehikoorma kingsepp, ja vanaema Hedwig õpetas talle, kuidas naaskel käes seisab.

Taniel Suvi is a shoemaker (Kriuks), a student of the Weifang master Wang Xiuyuan since 1991 and a 9th-generation inner-circle disciple of Taiji Tanglangquan, a freediving instructor and an underwater rescuer. The craft in the book runs in the family: his great-grandfather Artur was the shoemaker of Mehikoorma on Lake Peipus, and his grandmother Hedwig taught him how to hold an awl.

塔涅尔·苏维是鞋匠（Kriuks），自 1991 年起师从山东潍坊的王秀远先生，太极螳螂拳第九代入室弟子，自由潜水教练，也做水下救援。书里的手艺是家传的：外曾祖父阿尔图尔是佩普西湖边梅希科尔马村的鞋匠，外祖母海德薇格教会他锥子怎么拿。
